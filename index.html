Mock test series

Full GATE pattern: 65 questions, 100 marks, 1-mark and 2-mark questions, 1/3 negative marking on 1-mark MCQs and 2/3 on 2-mark MCQs (NAT questions have no negative marking). Mock 1 is ready now; Mocks 2–6 arrive as later subjects are built.

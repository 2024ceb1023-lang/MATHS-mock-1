It does not include official past-year GATE papers. For authentic previous-year question papers, refer to the official GATE portal at gate.iitm.ac.in.

Subject-wise combined sets

All topics of a subject shuffled together for integrated practice — this is how GATE actually mixes questions within a subject.

Additional practice questions

Questions modeled on the style of free/open resources (such as NPTEL assignment-style questions and open community test series), plus original questions written to cover additional ground. These are supplementary practice, not past-year papers.

Mock test series

Full GATE pattern: 65 questions, 100 marks, a mix of 1-mark and 2-mark questions, with 1/3 negative marking on wrong 1-mark answers and 2/3 on wrong 2-mark answers. Additional full-length tests are added as each subject's question bank is completed.
